# GHG-Performance-Analysis_DHL-Group_FY2021-FY2025.
This individual project is carried out to assess whether DHL Group is on track to achieve its science-based 2030 climate targets, and how well the public disclosures under ESRS E1 are prepared to verify that progress.

## Question
Is DHL Group on track to meet its SBTi-validated 2030 climate targets, and how well do its ESRS E1 disclosures are prepared to verify that progress?

## Short Answer
DHL is ahead of its total 2030 target on paper, but its own Scope 1+2 emissions are lagging behind. Most of the emission reduction came from lower volumes rather than decarbonization, and given the major contribution of Scope 3 emissions to DHL’s total carbon footprint, 80% of Scope 3 is still reported based on modeled data rather than measured data.

## Key findings
1. **Logistics-related total emissions fell 19.7% between the base year and 2025**, beating the straight-line value for 2025 (logistics-related: Scope 1, Scope 2 market-based, Scope 3 cat. 3, 4, 6). The decline was uneven (emissions rose 1.5% in 2024 as volumes recovered).
2. **Subcontracted transport accounts for 62% of DHL’s total footprint** The share of Scope 3 covered by the 2030 target fell from 85.0% in the base year to 80.1% in 2025, which they report as due to lower volumes and structural effects in road transport. Various decarbonization measures and efficiency improvements also led to the 21.1% reduction(2025) of Scope 3. 
3. **Two of the three targets are ahead and one is lagging behind.** Measured against an even pace (4 of 9 years = 44.4%), DHL has achieved a progress of  70.5% of the required reduction on the total and 84.5% on Scope 3, but only 31.9% on Scope 1+2. At 3% annual growth, 2030 emissions would reach 37.46 Mt without any new measures (illustrative assumption).
4. **Disclosure is broad but thin where it matters.** Only 19.9% of Scope 3 emissions are based on primary data, New GLEC 3.1 emission factors in 2025 raised ocean and road figures, but previous years were not stated to be recalculated., and there is no internal carbon pricing system

## Key numbers
